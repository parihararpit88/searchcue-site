SearchCue is a keyword research and analysis tool designed to help marketers organise search terms, understand search intent, discover recurring themes, and turn keyword data into clearer marketing insights.

Keyword demand

Review keyword datasets and search-volume estimates when available from supported data sources.

Intent and themes

Use AI-assisted classification to group keywords by intent, topic, and potential marketing need.

Actionable outputs

Explore results in a dashboard and export organised analysis for further planning.

Availability: SearchCue is currently in development for personal research and workflow testing. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Features and data availability may change as integrations are developed.
